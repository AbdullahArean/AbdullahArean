### Published & accepted

1. **[Stroke-Level Connectivity Verification: Grounding Vision-Language Models Against Topology Hallucination](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/abdullaharean_icdar2026-documentanalysis-visionlanguagemodels-activity-7462140293249650689-WpDO)** - *ICDAR 2026 (oral)*. A method for checking diagram stroke links so a model cannot invent connections.
2. **[SOMAJGYAAN: Evaluating LLMs on Bangla Culture and Social Knowledge](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-ijcnlp.134/)** - *AACL-IJCNLP 2025 Findings*. A 4,234-question benchmark for culturally grounded Bangla LLM evaluation. **Equal contribution.**
3. **LocalTurn: Native Multi-Turn Safety Evaluation in Emirati Arabic, Bangla, Sinhala, and Yoruba** - *NeurIPS 2026 Workshop, LP4FM*. Native, multi-turn safety evaluation across eight commercial LLMs.
